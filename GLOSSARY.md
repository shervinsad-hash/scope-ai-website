# Factory glossary

## Requirement

The desired outcome stated by the requester. It is not yet a technical design.

## Scope

The explicit boundary of a change: what is included and excluded.

## Specification

The reviewed description of user outcomes, prioritized stories, acceptance scenarios, edge cases,
requirements, assumptions, and measurable success criteria.

## Plan

The reviewed technical approach: project structure, architecture, boundaries, dependencies, risks,
constraints, and design decisions.

## Story

An independently implementable and testable unit of work with ownership, dependencies, and acceptance
criteria.

## Candidate

An isolated proposed change produced from an approved story. It is not merged or released.

## Verification

Evidence that compares an approved candidate with the approved requirements and acceptance criteria.

## Approval

A named human decision tied to the exact digest of an artifact. A passing test is not approval.

## Release

An explicit decision to merge, deploy, or activate a verified candidate. Release authority is separate
from implementation authority.

## Artifact digest

A canonical SHA-256 fingerprint of an artifact envelope. It identifies exactly what a reviewer approved.
